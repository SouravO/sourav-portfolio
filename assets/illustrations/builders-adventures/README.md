# Production comic illustrations

Generated with built-in imagegen from the user-approved concepts in `assets/concepts/builders-adventures/`. These are text-free illustration assets; the site supplies real HTML typography and controls.

- cover.png: corrected natural full-body standing pose, laptop under arm, parked car separate, panoramic street.
- cover-mobile.png: dedicated portrait recomposition of the corrected scene.
- deals.png: marketplace/phone discovery illustration; explanatory art, not a screenshot of the live product.
- crm.png: public-safe control-room/system illustration; not internal product data.
- workshop.png: red-shirt Sourav portrait at a drafting desk.
- finale.png: sunset street, character walking away, reserved sky for the contact UI.
- finale-mobile.png: dedicated portrait recomposition with larger sky area.

Art direction: thick black brush ink, faded vermilion, petrol blue, ochre and aged newsprint, Ben Day dots. Character likeness references are existing approved illustrations. No original personal photos are published.

Run `python3 scripts/prepare_adventure_assets.py` (Pillow) to create optimized WebP derivatives. Keep masters and past concepts unchanged. The script performs resizing and compression only.
