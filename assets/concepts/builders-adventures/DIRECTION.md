# Sourav: The Builder's Adventures

User supplied a worn urban adventure-comic cover as the visual authority. This concept supersedes the softer beige panel styling of PORT-004.

## Visual language
A collectible printed comic brought into the browser: dense confident brush ink, faded vermilion, petrol blue, newsprint cream, ochre captions, coarse Ben Day dots, and subtle registration imperfections. Sourav is the lead character, with curly black hair, mustache, short beard, red shirt and blue jeans. Cinematic street scenery forms the world, rather than a portrait floating beside marketing copy.

## Four concept sections
1. Cover: panoramic street scene, large tilted title, clear navigation, character by a car carrying a laptop, one Explore the work action.
2. Stories: asymmetric illustrated panels for Check Your Deals and Day One CRM; accurate status labels and case-study links; planning concepts stay separate.
3. Origin/workshop: portrait-led comic spread and an accessible interactive drafting-board workspace. Existing curated perspectives, terminal and chat remain real controls.
4. Contact: quieter oversized To be continued lettering, email action, panoramic street finale.

## Translation to implementation
Generated concepts are design references, not screenshots of implemented functionality. Rebuild all navigation, headings, text, links and controls as semantic HTML, with separate illustration assets; never ship a flattened screenshot as the UI. Keep ornamental distress outside body text and reserve ample caption contrast. On mobile, reframe the artwork and stack story panels; do not shrink a desktop composition. Use gentle scene parallax, panel reveals and hover ink offsets, with reduced-motion alternatives. Preserve current factual content, routing and API behavior.
