# Keyframe generation

Built-in image_gen, reference-guided generation. Originals retained under Codex generated_images; selected files copied into this directory. Sources: user-supplied office photo (facial likeness) and outdoor photo (overall likeness/style); other people ignored. Photos themselves are not published.

Preserve Sourav's facial structure, swept-up curly black hair, thick eyebrows, prominent mustache and short chin/jaw beard, and medium brown skin from supplied photos. Sophisticated 3D-meets-graphic-novel adult character, dimensional sculpted shapes, crisp ink contours, halftone shading, realistic proportions, not chibi. Three-quarter view toward laptop, hands at keyboard, thoughtful expression. Locked square framing; complete desk/body/feet visible with safe margins. Perfectly uniform magenta #FF00FF backdrop, no cast shadows/spill/gradient, text, UI or watermark. Controlled light, sharply defined opaque edges.

## End-frame prompt
